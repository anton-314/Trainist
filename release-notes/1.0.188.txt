What's new:

• Body measurements: a new card on the Stats screen — log neck, chest, waist, hips, biceps, thigh and calf in cm and follow their trend on a chart.
• Your measurements are part of backup export and import, so they move to a new device along with the rest of your data.
• Sets in a running workout can no longer be dragged around — they now stay in a fixed order.
