<en-US>
What's new:

• Food search by name: type what you're looking for instead of hunting for a barcode. Results stay limited to products sold in your country.
• New "Shopping country" setting — detected automatically, adjustable if you live abroad.
• Supersets: link exercises into a group, in a template or during a workout. The rest timer then starts only after the group's last exercise.
• Your own history and foods still come first in the search.
